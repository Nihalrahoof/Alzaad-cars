YOUR INFORMATION

Privacy notice.

When you submit an enquiry, Al Zaad Cars receives the details you provide, such as your name, phone number, email, message and vehicle information. These details are used to respond to your request and discuss a viewing, purchase, sale or trade-in.

How enquiries are handled

Enquiries are processed through Google Apps Script and stored in a private Google Sheet accessible to authorised showroom staff. The public inventory service exposes only published vehicle listing fields, not enquiry records. The website does not accept payments or ask for identity documents.

External services

WhatsApp, Google Maps, Instagram and Facebook links open services with their own privacy practices. If you provide an external photo album link, anyone with access to that link may be able to view the images. Do not include personal documents or information you do not want shared.

If anti-spam verification is enabled, Cloudflare Turnstile processes information needed to distinguish legitimate submissions from abuse. Website and service providers may process technical logs when you visit or submit a form.

Your choices

You can contact us by phone or email instead of using the form. To request access, correction or deletion of your enquiry details, email sales@alzaadcars.com. Enquiry information should be kept only as long as needed to respond and meet applicable obligations.
